## Step 1

delay_days: 0
notes: Theo dials. Let them talk after each question.

10 second open
"Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{sender_name}}. I work with full-time Philadelphia landlords with a dozen or so properties. Ten repair texts a week with no record, summer turnover and chasing contractors and rent are what I hear most. We put all of that in one place. Do you have two minutes?"

Three questions (let them talk)

1. Walk me through what happens when a repair text comes in. Who sees it, who fixes it, where does the record end up?
2. How did last turnover season go? What fell through the cracks?
3. When tax time or a license renewal comes up, where do you dig for the receipts and the dates?

The ask
"Worth 15 minutes on a screen so you can see it with your own properties? It's free while we're in beta, plus two months free after the public launch. Nothing to sign. Here is the link: {{demo_link}}"

If they say "I tried software before, it was more work than the spreadsheet"
"Most of it is built for 2,000 units. This is built for {{property_count}} rowhomes with you running them. The 15 minutes shows you if that is true."

If they say "Not now, it's turnover season"
"Fair. Turnover season is the worst time to change anything. When does it slow down? I'll call then."
