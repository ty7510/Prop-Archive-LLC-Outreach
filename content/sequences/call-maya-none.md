## Step 1

delay_days: 0
notes: Theo dials. Let them talk after each question.

10 second open
"Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{sender_name}}. I work with Philadelphia owners who have a day job and a few rentals. The repair text that lands at work and tax time with no records are the two things I hear most. We put all of that in one place. Do you have two minutes?"

Three questions (let them talk)

1. When a repair text comes in during your work day, what happens next?
2. How did tax time go this year? Where were the receipts and the rent records?
3. How do you keep track of the rental license and lead cert dates for your {{property_count}} places?

The ask
"Worth 15 minutes on a screen so you can see it with your own places? It's free while we're in beta, plus two months free after the public launch. Nothing to sign. Here is the link: {{demo_link}}"

If they say "I just use my phone and it works"
"It works until a tenant disputes a repair or the license lapses. The 15 minutes is to see what one place for all of it looks like."

If they say "Not now, busy"
"Fair. What week is less busy? I'll call back then." Then set the date.
