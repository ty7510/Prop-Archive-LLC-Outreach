## Step 1

delay_days: 0
notes: Theo dials by hand. Only call someone who has talked with you or asked about Prop Archive; Pennsylvania treats any other sales call as telemarketing that needs registration. If that talk was more than 3 months ago, check the Do Not Call list first. 9 AM to 7 PM, never Sunday or a legal holiday. Take no payment on the phone. Let them talk after each question.

10 second open
"Hi {{first_name}}, this is {{sender_name}}, calling about how you keep the records for your rentals. The repair text that lands at work and tax time with the receipts all over your phone are the two things I hear most. We put every text, receipt and paper for each place in one search. Do you have two minutes?"

Three questions (let them talk)

1. When a repair text comes in during your work day, what happens next?
2. How did tax time go this year? Where were the receipts?
3. If a tenant asked for your rental license or lead cert tomorrow, where would you look?

The ask
"Worth 15 minutes on a screen so you can see it with your own places? It's free while we're in beta, plus two months free after the public launch. Nothing to sign. Here is the link: {{demo_link}}"

If they say "I just use my phone and it works"
"It works until a tenant disputes a repair from six months ago. The 15 minutes is to see what one search for all of it looks like."

If they say "Not now, busy"
"Fair. What week is less busy? I'll call back then." Then set the date.
